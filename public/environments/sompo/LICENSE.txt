Vegetation:
Tree Small 02 — Rico Cilliers — https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02
The three transparent tree-*.png billboards were rendered locally by LUCA-AI
from the CC0 glTF model using its bark, branch, leaf and leaf-alpha textures.
They are not the website's copyrighted preview renders.
Adaptations: three camera angles, lighting and reduced-resolution rasterization
for instanced roadside vegetation.
