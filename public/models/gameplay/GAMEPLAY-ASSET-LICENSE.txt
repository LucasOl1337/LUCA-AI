Gameplay assets (simulação em primeira pessoa do Trator MAX)
============================================================

Files covered:
- max-tractor-cabine.glb (original procedural geometry and PBR materials of the
  MAX TRATOR toy, authored for LUCA-AI by scripts/sompo/build-max-tractor.py
  with MAX_TRACTOR_VARIANT=cabine)

It is the same toy model as ../sompo/max-tractor.glb (see
SOMPO-AGRI-ASSET-LICENSE.txt there), with the old dashboard bar, seat and
steering wheel removed and a new procedural steering wheel in its own node
(rig-volante). It references the owner's own photos of the toy in
missoes/trator-referencias, used only for proportion, colour and part
placement. No photo, image texture or downloaded geometry is embedded.

Each .provenance.json records the construction method, measured triangle
count, output hash, steering-wheel pivot and known limits.

Tripo Studio props
------------------
- cabine-console.glb, cabine-banco.glb, fardo-feno.glb, cabine-volante.glb and
  cabine-teto.glb, with their -basecolor/-normal/-metallicroughness.jpg
  textures, were generated from text prompts in Tripo Studio
  (studio.tripo3d.ai) with the owner's paid subscription, which grants
  commercial use of the generated models. They were decimated and their atlases
  reduced by scripts/gameplay/package-tripo-props.py. Each .provenance.json
  records the Tripo project, prompt, source hash and packaging.

Poly Haven texture
------------------
- cabine-detalhe-couro.jpg packs the OpenGL normal (R, G) and roughness (B) of
  "Fabric Leather 02" by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_02), 1k JPG, licensed CC0 1.0
  (public domain). Used as a triplanar detail map on the cabin plastics in
  src/components/gameplay/visual/materialCabine.ts.
