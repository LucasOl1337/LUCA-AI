Gameplay assets (simulação em primeira pessoa do Trator MAX)
============================================================

Files covered:
- max-tractor-cabine.glb (original procedural geometry and PBR materials of the
  MAX TRATOR toy, authored for LUCA-AI by scripts/sompo/build-max-tractor.py
  with MAX_TRACTOR_VARIANT=cabine)

It is the same toy model as ../sompo/max-tractor.glb (see
SOMPO-AGRI-ASSET-LICENSE.txt there), with the old dashboard bar, seat and
steering wheel removed and a new procedural steering wheel in its own node
(rig-volante). It references the owner's own photos of the toy in
missoes/trator-referencias, used only for proportion, colour and part
placement. No photo, image texture or downloaded geometry is embedded.

Each .provenance.json records the construction method, measured triangle
count, output hash, steering-wheel pivot and known limits.
