Sompo agricultural machine assets
=================================

Files covered:
- generated-agri-tractor-source.png and generated-agri-tractor.glb, including
  the external texture images named in generated-agri-tractor.textures.json
- generated-agri-harvester-source.png and generated-agri-harvester.glb

The source images were generated for LUCA-AI through the locally authorized
9Router route cx/gpt-image-2 on 2026-09-11. Their prompts intentionally request
unbranded equipment and remain recorded in the reconstruction pipeline history.
Use of those generated images remains subject to the applicable image-provider
terms.

The tractor geometry and PBR atlases were reconstructed locally with Tencent
Hunyuan3D-2.1. Redistribution and use of that reconstructed output remains
subject to the Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.1 Community License preserved in this
directory as HUNYUAN-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.txt.

The harvester geometry is an original, unbranded procedural hard-surface model
created for LUCA-AI in Blender by scripts/sompo/build-agri-harvester.py. Its
generated source image was used only as a visual reference; the mesh and PBR
materials contain no Hunyuan reconstruction output and no downloaded geometry.

Each .provenance.json records the applicable source or reference, construction
method, measured triangle count, output hash and known limits.
