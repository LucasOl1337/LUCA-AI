Sompo agricultural machine assets
=================================

Files covered:
- generated-agri-tractor-source.png (visual reference)
- generated-agri-tractor.glb (original procedural geometry and PBR materials,
  authored for LUCA-AI by scripts/sompo/build-agri-tractor.py)
- generated-agri-harvester-source.png (visual reference)
- generated-agri-harvester.glb (original procedural geometry and PBR materials,
  authored for LUCA-AI by scripts/sompo/build-agri-harvester.py)

The source images were generated for LUCA-AI through the locally authorized
9Router route cx/gpt-image-2 on 2026-09-11. Their prompts intentionally request
unbranded equipment. Use of those generated images remains subject to the
applicable image-provider terms. They are used only as visual references.

Both machines are original, unbranded procedural hard-surface models created
for LUCA-AI in Blender. Their meshes and PBR materials contain no Hunyuan3D
reconstruction output, no image textures and no downloaded geometry. Earlier
releases shipped Hunyuan3D-2.1 reconstructions of these machines; that output
is no longer distributed here. HUNYUAN-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.txt stays in this
directory for the other reconstructed assets that still use it.

Each .provenance.json records the applicable reference, construction method,
measured triangle count, output hash and known limits.
