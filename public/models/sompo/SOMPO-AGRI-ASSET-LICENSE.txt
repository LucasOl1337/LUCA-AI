Sompo agricultural machine assets
=================================

Files covered:
- generated-agri-tractor-source.png (visual reference)
- generated-agri-tractor.glb (original procedural geometry and PBR materials,
  authored for LUCA-AI by scripts/sompo/build-agri-tractor.py)
- generated-agri-harvester-source.png and generated-agri-harvester.glb,
  including the external texture images named in
  generated-agri-harvester.textures.json

The source images were generated for LUCA-AI through the locally authorized
9Router route cx/gpt-image-2 on 2026-09-11. The prompts intentionally request
unbranded equipment and are recorded verbatim in each provenance JSON. Use of
those generated images remains subject to the applicable image-provider terms.

The harvester geometry and PBR atlases were reconstructed locally with Tencent
Hunyuan3D-2.1. Redistribution and use of that reconstructed output remains
subject to the Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.1 Community License preserved in this
directory as HUNYUAN-COMMUNITY-LICENSE.txt. The tractor GLB is not Hunyuan
output: it is reproducible original Blender geometry with no image textures.

Each .provenance.json records the applicable source or method, measured
triangle count, output hash and known limits.
