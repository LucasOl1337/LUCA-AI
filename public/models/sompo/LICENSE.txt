Generated Nelore — generated-nelore.glb
Created locally for LUCA-AI, 2026-09-11. Source image generated with
cx/gpt-5.5-image through local 9Router; shape and PBR generated locally with
Tencent Hunyuan3D-2.1. Same tool license terms preserved above.
Adaptations: mesh reduced to 65,000 triangles; nonmetallic hide; calibrated
animal size and procedural leg/tail movement. Not an anatomically rigged scan.
Source image and full parameters: generated-nelore.provenance.json.

External texture copies (Chrome compatibility):
The *.textures.json manifests bind byte-identical JPEG/PNG copies extracted
from each GLB to their original glTF image indices. TextureLoader loads those
files through HTMLImageElement after parsing, with glTF UV orientation and
sRGB only for color/emissive maps. Original asset credits and terms also apply
to the extracted Tesla images; extraction does not change their license.

Generated trees — generated-jacaranda.glb, generated-eucalyptus.glb,
and generated-cerrado.glb (ipê/cerrado reference).
Created locally for LUCA-AI, 2026-09-11. Botanical source images generated
with cx/gpt-image-2 through local 9Router; shape and PBR textures generated
with Tencent Hunyuan3D-2.1 under the same preserved tool license above.
Each mesh is reduced to 18,000 triangles before texture baking. Source
images, generation parameters, limits and hashes accompany each GLB as
*.provenance.json. Textures also ship separately through *.textures.json.
Roadside instances share the real mesh and atlas; image impostors are used
only beyond 65 metres. Fine foliage and unseen surfaces are inferred from
one reference image, rather than an individually modelled botanical scan.

CSP compatibility: glTF parsing constructs temporary texture slots instead of
fetching embedded blob URLs (blocked by production connect-src). External
maps replace those slots before the asset is attached. No CSP relaxation is
needed. Tesla geometry expansion preserves the decoded data and original
embedded image bytes; source/result hashes: tesla-semi.provenance.json.
